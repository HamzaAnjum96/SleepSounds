## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM assessment: does not read as AI-made. A committed nocturnal identity (Cormorant
italic over Inter, a living seeded starfield, one cool accent, warm gold reserved
for the timer and saved things) applied with restraint. The tells that remain are
the density of tiny tracked uppercase labels, which is a deliberate system here
(uppercase means static label), and decorative glows, which carry state.

Deterministic scan: CLI found 2 (both judged): `layout-transition` on the card
volume reveal (`max-height`, src/index.css), real but small; `single-font` in
index.html, a false positive (the noscript fallback). In-page: 54-61 findings
per view, dominated by `clipped-overflow-container` (29, intentional clipping of
card decoration) and `layout-transition` (19, the same rule per card); also
`ai-color-palette` (aurora indigo/violet/teal at <=0.10 alpha), `dark-glow`
(moon halo, accent glow on active state), `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` (active
card glow), `tiny-text` (10px body text: confirmed real, see P1),
`italic-serif-display` and `overused-font` (the brand pairing), and two
`text-overflow` false positives (visually-hidden elements). The detector missed
both P0/P1 visual defects below; they needed pixel measurement.

## Priority Issues

- [P1] Frosted glass never rendered on Chrome or Firefox. Lightning CSS (Vite 8)
  keeps only the last of a property and its -webkit- twin; standard-first source
  order shipped only -webkit-backdrop-filter. Since 0.0.38 page text read through
  the sheet, toast and mini player. Fix: prefixed first, standard last; e2e guard.
- [P1] Reading text at label size. Scene moods, the sheet's fade note and the
  mini-player subtitle (the countdown) were 10px; the scene layer count 9px.
  Fix: sentences to body size (12px), readouts to 11px, labels stay 10px.
- [P2] Muddy scene art (Curled Up, Evening Porch): warm gold spread thin over the
  night-blue reads brown and cancels against the cool glow. Chroma 0.011-0.013
  vs 0.018-0.032; 16% khaki pixels on Evening Porch. Fix: concentrated warm core
  in its own corner, cool ink base. Same mechanism in saved-mix tints (~0.010).
- [P3] Scene names misaligned across a row once a mood wraps to three lines
  (exposed by the larger mood text). Fix: top-anchor the name, pin the count.
- [P3] Em dashes in user-facing copy (save failure toast, editor fallback,
  offline badge). Fix: commas and semicolons.

## Persona Red Flags

Night user (project persona: in bed, lights off, half-asleep): the countdown and
mood lines at 10px were the first things to blur; sentences at label size.
Sam (accessibility): solid. Landmarks, live region, focus rings, forced-colors
block, axe gate. The toast lacked the reduced-transparency fallback the other
glass surfaces had.
Casey (one-handed mobile): switching a sound on grows its grid row (~28px,
animated max-height), so the next card moves while the thumb aims at it.

## Minor Observations

- Category filters wrap to three rows on a 393px phone with "Urban" alone on the
  last row (and orphaned on desktop); a deliberate earlier choice, left as is.
- The aurora's violet/teal sits close to the PRODUCT.md anti-reference
  ("purple-teal gradients"), though at <=0.10 alpha it barely registers.
- Close glyphs mix a text "✕" (cards, toast, layer rows) with the Material
  "close" icon (editor).

## Questions to Consider

- Should the card volume reveal reserve its row, trading ~280px of scroll for a
  grid that never moves under the thumb?
- Is "sleep-safe" the right name for a master tone setting most users will never
  touch, or should it live inside the sheet's master row as a plain switch?
